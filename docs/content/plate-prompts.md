# Far Side Protocol: plate prompts

One prompt per plate. Each is complete on its own: copy the whole block.

How the plates are used: the game shows the picture as the background of a station and
draws the moving things on top (lamps, the open gate, people, papers, the interface). So
every plate must be **empty, idle and free of text**, and must keep certain areas calm
because the interface sits there.

Size: ask for 16:9 landscape at the largest size the tool offers. Anything 1920 × 1080 or
larger is ideal; smaller is fine, it will be scaled. If the tool only makes square
pictures, make the widest it allows and it will be cropped.

Order of need: 1 Gate hall first. Then 2 Alien panel. 3 to 5 come with later phases.
6 is optional.

---

## 1. Gate hall (Gate station)

```
A wide establishing view of an underground military gate hall, used as a background
for a strategy game. 16:9 landscape.

CAMERA: Eye level, standing on a control balcony at the back of the hall, looking
straight ahead at the far wall. Symmetrical one-point perspective. Fixed, level
horizon, no tilt, no fisheye.

SUBJECT, CENTRE: A large upright metal ring standing on a low steel platform against
the far wall. The ring is exactly centred left to right, and its centre is a little
above the middle of the picture. Its outer diameter is a little under half the height
of the picture. The ring is a thick band of dull gunmetal, perfectly circular, seen
flat-on. Eight heavy rectangular clamp blocks are bolted around its outer edge at even
spacing, like the points of a compass, each with a small dark unlit lamp. The face of
the band carries a row of small simple engraved marks. The ring is switched off: its
inside is EMPTY and you see the plain dark concrete wall behind it through the hole.
No glow, no energy, no water, no light inside the ring.

A wide steel ramp with low handrails runs from the foot of the ring straight toward the
camera down the middle of the floor, ending about two thirds of the way down the picture.

THE HALL: A tall hall cut into rock and lined with poured concrete and riveted steel
plates. Large simple forms. Steel catwalks with railings run along the left and right
walls at mid height, with one steel staircase on the left. A heavy sliding blast door,
closed, in the left wall at floor level. Two low banks of plain equipment cabinets, one
on each side of the ramp, with dark switched-off screens. Thick cable trays and a few
large pipes along the ceiling. Yellow and black hazard stripes painted on the floor
around the ramp. The concrete floor is matte and dry, with painted lines.

LIGHT: Five or six industrial lamps hanging from the ceiling throw warm sodium-orange
pools of light on the floor and ramp. The corners and the upper walls fall into deep
shadow. A few cool white strip lights under the catwalks. Overall dim and moody but
every large shape readable.

KEEP CALM AND DARK (interface goes here): the bottom fifth of the picture is plain dark
floor with nothing important on it. The top tenth is dark ceiling. The right fifth of
the picture is darker and less detailed than the left.

ART STYLE: Stylised pre-rendered 3D, like the background of a late 1990s PC adventure
or strategy game. Low to medium detail. Blunt, chunky, simple geometry; flat matte
surfaces with light wear and chipped paint; baked soft shadows. Restricted palette:
concrete grey-green, olive drab, gunmetal, rust brown, with sodium orange as the only
warm accent. No blue anywhere. Slight film grain and a faint dither. Not photorealistic,
not glossy, no mirror reflections, no lens flare, no bloom, no fog, no depth-of-field
blur. Clean silhouettes.

MUST NOT CONTAIN: people, figures, vehicles, text, letters, numbers, signs, logos,
flags, banners, emblems, user interface, frames, borders, watermarks.
```

## 2. Alien panel (compose an address)

```
A straight-on view of the face of an ancient alien control panel, used as a full-screen
background for a game interface. 16:9 landscape.

CAMERA: Looking perfectly square at the panel from the front, like a scan of a flat
object. No perspective, no tilt.

SUBJECT: A huge circular dial carved from dark bronze-grey stone-like metal, centred,
filling almost the full height of the picture. It is made of three concentric rings
around a round central boss. Each ring is a smooth, slightly sunken channel, EMPTY:
plain bare surface with no buttons, no keys, no symbols and no markings on it (the game
draws the keys on top). Thin raised ridges separate the rings. The central boss is a
plain shallow dome, also blank. Fine cracks, pitting and worn edges show great age.
A few thin inlaid lines of dull tarnished gold run between the rings.

Left and right of the dial, the panel continues as plain flat slabs of the same
material, mostly blank, with a few shallow rectangular recesses, also empty. The left
slab is the plainest area (a document is shown there).

LIGHT: Soft light from above and slightly to the left, raking across the surface so
the ridges and channels read clearly. A very faint warm amber glow seeps from the gaps
between the rings. Edges of the picture fall into shadow.

ART STYLE: Stylised pre-rendered 3D, like the background of a late 1990s PC adventure
game. Low to medium detail, blunt simple forms, matte surfaces, baked soft shadows.
Restricted palette: dark bronze, grey-brown stone, tarnished gold, deep shadow. No blue.
Slight film grain and a faint dither. Not photorealistic, not glossy, no lens flare,
no bloom, no fog.

MUST NOT CONTAIN: symbols, glyphs, runes, letters, numbers, writing of any kind,
buttons, keys, screens, hands, people, user interface, borders, watermarks.
```

## 3. Plan table (Base station)

```
A top-down view of a large steel planning table in an underground operations room,
used as a background for a strategy game. 16:9 landscape.

CAMERA: Directly overhead, looking straight down. No perspective, no tilt.

SUBJECT: A heavy industrial table that fills the picture. Most of its top, about three
quarters of the picture, centred slightly left, is one large recessed panel of dark
matte glass with a very faint fine grid etched into it. The glass is EMPTY and switched
off: no map, no drawing, no light on it (the game draws the base plan there).

Around the glass is a thick frame of grey-green painted steel with large bolts at the
corners and worn, chipped edges. Along the right edge of the frame, a flat steel margin
about a sixth of the picture wide, plain and empty. Along the bottom edge, a shallow
steel tray running the full width, empty.

On the frame, a few small props near the corners only: a pencil, a steel ruler, a dented
enamel mug, a brass desk lamp arm reaching in from the top left corner. Nothing on the
glass itself.

LIGHT: One warm sodium-orange lamp from the top left throws a soft pool across the frame
and a long soft shadow from the mug. The glass stays dark. Corners fall into shadow.

ART STYLE: Stylised pre-rendered 3D, like the background of a late 1990s PC adventure
or strategy game. Low to medium detail, blunt chunky geometry, flat matte surfaces with
light wear, baked soft shadows. Restricted palette: concrete grey-green, olive drab,
gunmetal, rust brown, sodium orange accent. No blue. Slight film grain and a faint
dither. Not photorealistic, not glossy, no reflections on the glass, no lens flare,
no bloom.

MUST NOT CONTAIN: maps, blueprints, drawings, papers with writing, text, letters,
numbers, logos, hands, people, user interface, borders, watermarks.
```

SUBJECT: The far wall is almost entirely one huge matte dark-green board in a steel

To the right of the board, a tall steel shelf with a few sealed crates and plain

## 5. Director's desk (Office station)

```
A view down onto a director's desk in a small underground office, 1990s government
facility, used as a background for a strategy game. 16:9 landscape.

CAMERA: Seated at the desk, looking down at the desktop at about 50 degrees, so the
desktop fills most of the picture and a strip of the back wall shows along the top.
Level, no tilt.

SUBJECT: A wide steel-and-linoleum desk. Four objects on it, well separated, each
clearly readable and unobstructed, with empty desk surface between them:
1. Far left: a heavy red rotary-dial telephone with a coiled cord.
2. Left of centre: a two-tier steel paper tray, both tiers EMPTY.
3. Centre and right of centre: a large green cloth-bound ledger, CLOSED, lying flat,
   with a plain blank cover. A clear empty area of desk in front of it, about a third
   of the picture wide (papers are shown there by the game).
4. Far right, on the floor beside the desk, the top of a grey steel filing cabinet with
   its top drawer closed and a blank label holder.

Small props only at the back edge of the desk: a brass desk lamp, an ashtray, a mug
with pencils. The back wall is painted concrete with one steel conduit and an empty
cork noticeboard.

LIGHT: The brass lamp throws a warm sodium-orange pool over the middle of the desk.
The rest of the room is dim. Soft long shadows from the objects.

ART STYLE: Stylised pre-rendered 3D, like the background of a late 1990s PC adventure
game. Low to medium detail, blunt chunky geometry, flat matte surfaces with light wear,
baked soft shadows. Restricted palette: concrete grey-green, olive drab, gunmetal,
off-white, cork brown, one red object (the telephone), sodium orange light. No blue.
Slight film grain and a faint dither. Not photorealistic, not glossy, no lens flare,
no bloom.

MUST NOT CONTAIN: papers with writing, open books, text, letters, numbers, labels,
logos, photographs, screens, computers, people, hands, user interface, borders,
watermarks.
```

## Notes

- If the tool adds text, flags or people anyway, say so in a follow-up ("remove the
  banners; leave the wall bare") rather than starting again; keeping one room is worth
  more than a perfect first try.
- For plate 1 the three things that matter most are: the ring is centred and flat-on,
  its inside is empty and dark, and the bottom fifth is plain floor.
- The Shutter, the open gate, the lamps, people and papers are drawn by the game, so
  none of them should be in a plate.
- If the style drifts between plates, add to the next prompt: "Match the style, palette
  and level of detail of the attached image" and attach plate 1.
