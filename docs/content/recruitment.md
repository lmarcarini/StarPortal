# Recruitment

Design agreed with the owner on 2026-11-20. Not yet carded; it is built with the Office
(interface phase 4), where recruitment lives.

## Owner decisions

- **Hiring is instant.** The person walks in from the lift the same day.
- **The candidate board refreshes as a whole** on a timer (starting every 7 days, with the
  countdown shown); candidates no longer leave one by one.
- **No signing fee:** a hire costs only a **daily salary**, charged with the other daily
  costs. Beds still cap headcount.
- **Five fields**, each with its own colour and icon:

  | Field | Today's role | Does |
  | --- | --- | --- |
  | Military | Soldier | Squads, security, expeditions |
  | Office | Analyst (the role keeps its name) | Committee and Confidence, documents, translation |
  | Science | Scientist | Research, artefact studies |
  | Engineering | Engineer | Building, power, repairs, the MOTH |
  | Medicine | Doctor (new) | Infirmary: treating injuries, recovery speed; later disease and contagion |

  Each candidate card shows the field and small icons of the rooms the person would boost.
  Specialities within a field (medic, linguist, demolitions) can come later.
- **No skill shown without HR.** Until a Personnel Office is built, candidate cards show
  field and name only, no skill at all. With it, skill and one trait are shown.
- **Tiers come from HR research.** Building the Personnel Office makes the HR research
  projects available; they unlock better candidates and a better board:

  | Research | Effect |
  | --- | --- |
  | Headhunter network I / II | Candidate tier 2 (skill 2–4), tier 3 (skill 3–5, specialists) |
  | Recruitment drive I / II | Candidates per batch 3 → 5 → 7 |
  | Fast-track I / II | Refresh every 7 → 5 → 3 days |

  Tier 1 (skill 1–2) is the starting pool.
- **Aliens (a surprise, kept out of playtest guides):** at Allied standing a faction's envoy
  may offer a recruit, shown on the board as a special card, possibly with unique skills
  or unusual needs.

## Open for the cards

- Where the HR research sits in the new research tree (the tree is being redesigned).

## Personnel (owner decisions 2026-11-20)

**Tiers.** Recruit, Specialist and Veteran can be hired (better tiers through HR research);
**Expert** cannot be hired, only reached by training, and slowly decays unless kept up.
Eligibility for promotion comes automatically with experience; the player chooses when to
promote, because promotion means days away in a training facility.

**Attributes and trait (fixed).** Four attributes rated 1–5, used differently by each
field and on expeditions:

| Attribute | In the base | On expeditions |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Mind | Research speed, translation | Finding things, reading ruins |
| Hands | Building, repairs, the MOTH | Salvage, field camps, artefact recovery |
| Nerve | Defence, incidents | Survival in fights and disasters |
| Presence | Committee calls, recruitment, trade | Contact with aliens |

Plus one trait, usually with a twist (Polyglot, Night owl, Claustrophobic, Lucky,
Ex-Continuity as a later story hook).

**Expeditions.** Any mix of people up to the team size. Each field brings something
obvious: Military survival and fights; Science finds and specimens; Engineering salvage,
artefact recovery and camps; Medicine field healing; Office contact, trade and documents.
The leader's field sets which mission types can be chosen; attributes and traits add
their part.

**Fatigue (light).** Work, expeditions and incidents tire people; tired people work slower
and make more mistakes. Quarters and a **Cantina** restore them (the Cantina later hosts
staff events). A **Training Centre** runs promotions and Expert upkeep; a **Range** lets
Military staff train while idle. No morale system.

**Looks.** Procedural **pixel-art** portraits (owner's reference: a Probably Stolen mod on
Nexus Mods) built from parts: face shape, skin, gender, hair, eyes, nose, mouth, age and
one mark (scar, tattoo, glasses, earring, freckles), uniform by field. In 3D the same
people appear as 2D pixel sprites standing in the 3D hall (2D-HD style).
