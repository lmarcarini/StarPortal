## 1. Puzzle 5: The Iron Hook

**Delivered:** on any STILLWATER lock, by the MOTH. **Look around** lists two targets, "Bare
footprints by the water" and "Iron object at the causeway's end". A **Close look** at the iron
object (4 min of battery, no loss chance on a LOW world) adds two documents to the Archive: the
photo (5A) and the copied marks (5B). A Recover (1 day) later brings the object itself (5C);
it is not needed for the puzzle.

**Teaches:** an address written on another world carries that world's own mark as its eighth
seal; a count can be written in a notation the player has to work out from the object itself.

**Why it is a step above puzzle 4:** two tricks instead of one, and nothing in the text says
what either is. Puzzle 4 told the player there were seven verses and that the refrain did not
count; here there are eight marks for seven slots, and the order is in a notation no document
explains.

### What the player holds

**Document 5A: MOTH close look, the iron object** (image, with the MOTH operator's log)

> MOTH 1. STILLWATER. CLOSE LOOK: IRON OBJECT.
>
> Range 2 m. The object is an anchor, iron, hand-forged, about 1.6 m long: a ring at the top,
> a crossbar (stock) under it, a straight shank, and two curved arms at the foot. It stands
> crown-down at the end of the causeway, leaning toward the Ring. The right arm is under the
> water; the water is clear and perfectly still, and the arm can be seen through it.
>
> Small marks are punched into the iron, eight in all, each with a row of cuts beside it. Some
> rows are short cuts only; some start with one long cut. Marks traced: see the copy.
>
> [Operator: rust on everything except the marks. Someone keeps them clean.]

**What it shows.** A camcorder still, close and slightly from above, scanlines over it. The
anchor fills the frame, the causeway's fitted stones at the left edge, the still water at the
bottom right with the sunk arm pale beneath its surface.

**Document 5B: Marks copied: the iron object** (image: the drone's tracing, each mark drawn as
a glyph-like line drawing in the place it sits, with its row of cuts)

| Where on the anchor | Mark | Cuts beside it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Stock, left end | Two long rails leaning together, joined by short crossbars | 3 short |
| Stock, right end | A tall frame with a slab swung half open and a dot near its edge | 1 long |
| Shank, top | A flat band with three tall points rising from it | 1 long, 1 short |
| Shank, middle | Square notches climbing to one side | 1 short |
| Shank, low | A body with two pairs of wings | 4 short |
| Crown, where the arms meet, inside a punched circle | A seat with a straight back and four legs | 1 long, 3 short |
| Left arm | A thick triangle, broad at one end and thin at the other | 1 long, 2 short |
| Right arm (under the water) | A tall upright stroke with two arms bending upward | 2 short |

The tracing shows the cuts plainly: short cuts are about a finger wide apart; a long cut is
three times the length of a short one.

**From earlier puzzles and probes, already in the Archive:**

- Order No. 1 (puzzle 1): the sill stone's seven carvings, then "the eighth is the Ring's own
  mark, which the console sets."
- STILLWATER's MOTH Look around (section 2, MOTH texts): "On the flat stone before the Ring,
  one mark cut alone: a seat with a straight back."
- The dial console: seal 8 always shows CAIRN's own mark and cannot be changed.

### Solution

1. The cuts are a count. Four marks have 1 to 4 short cuts; the other four start with one long
   cut and add 0 to 3 short ones. Each count appears once, so a long cut is five, as in a tally:
   long = 5, long and 1 = 6, long and 2 = 7, long and 3 = 8.
2. In count order: Stairs (1), Cactus (2), Ladder (3), Butterfly (4), Doorway (5), Crown (6),
   Wedge (7), Chair (8).
3. Eight marks, seven slots. The eighth, set apart in a circle at the crown, is the Chair: the
   mark cut alone before STILLWATER's Ring, so STILLWATER's own mark. Order No. 1 said the
   eighth is the Ring's own mark. The Kindred wrote the way home as it is dialed from
   STILLWATER; from CAIRN the console sets its own eighth.
4. The address is Stairs, Cactus, Ladder, Butterfly, Doorway, Crown, Wedge. The lock needs
   366.6 MWh. The player dials and locks MOORING.

Expected time: eight to fifteen minutes. The two tricks do not depend on each other, and either
one alone gives a clear "something is off" signal (ties, or one mark too many).

**How each wrong reading fails.**

| Wrong reading | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| By position, top to bottom (Ladder first) | The console will not turn: every address not starting with the Stairs needs more than one bank. No charge spent |
| A long cut read as one | Four pairs of marks share a count; there is no order to dial |
| The Chair kept, the Wedge dropped (35, 19, 3, 24, 22, 5, 33) | No gate (36.7 MWh); no point in space has this address |
| A look-alike (see "Decoys") | No gate (36.7 MWh) |
| Butterfly and Doorway swapped, or Crown and Wedge swapped | No gate (36.7 MWh); see "Decoys" for why these two need the generator's help |

### Answer

**Seals 1 to 7:** Stairs, Cactus, Ladder, Butterfly, Doorway, Crown, Wedge (glyphs 35, 19, 3,
24, 22, 5, 29). Seal 8: CAIRN's mark. From STILLWATER (origin glyph 33, the Chair) the same
seven are followed by the Chair, as on the anchor.

### Document 5C: The iron hook (Recover)

A team that recovers the anchor brings it home: an object document with three images (whole,
the crown circle, the stock) and the Codex entry "The iron hook". Its text adds one thing the
drone could not see: on the underside of the stock, scratched rather than punched, "OF THE LONG
HEARTH" in plain capitals, a drifted English spelling the analyst notes ("hearth" spelt
*harth*). The Recover also sets the flag `anchor_recovered` (card 19 puts it on the target's
data), which K2 reads. A recovered anchor stays a document; its marks stay readable in 5B.

### Position and checks

Placed with a throwaway Python script (outside the repository) that reads
`data/gate/home_anchor_field.tres` and applies the address rule as m15 did (exact integer
squared distances, ties to the lower glyph), searched on a 100 ly grid over the anchor slab,
then refined on a 10 ly grid inside the chosen region. Card 19 should confirm it with the Godot
validator.

| World | Position (ly) | Address from CAIRN | Lock energy | From CAIRN | Smallest gap between anchor distances |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| MOORING | (-5010, 1340, -360) | 35, 19, 3, 24, 22, 5, 29, 2 | 366.6 MWh | 4,821 ly | 252 ly |

- **Dialable:** seal 1 is glyph 35 (2,220.7 ly from CAIRN), so the lock needs 366.6 MWh.
- **The far-side constraint holds:** the nine nearest anchors are 35, 19, 3, 24, 22, 5, 29, 10,
  34; neither STILLWATER's origin (33) nor CAIRN's (2) is among the first seven, so the anchor's
  seven are MOORING's address from both Rings.
- **Unique:** for every origin 0 to 35, MOORING's address differs from CAIRN's, SILL's,
  LINTEL's, MILESTONE's and STILLWATER's.
- **Seal by seal:** it differs from SILL, MILESTONE and STILLWATER in all of seals 2 to 7, and
  from LINTEL in all but seal 2 (the Cactus). No reachable region with a sizeable volume
  differs from all four in every seal.
- **Neighbours:** the nearest authored world is MILESTONE, 1,518 ly away; the two share six
  glyphs (all but the Crown and the Candle). The hint table uses this on purpose.
- **Spacing:** 2,875 ly from the nearest anchor, well past the 50 ly minimum.
- **MOORING's own origin glyph:** 13, the Fish (not among its seven).

## 2. MOORING

**The world.** A Kindred fishing village on a cold grey sea. The Ring stands at the landward
end of a stone quay; a shingle beach curves away with boats drawn up on it, and above it a long
turf-roofed hearth-house with smoke rising. Kindred fish STILLWATER's lake by Ring, which is
why their anchor lies there: the way home cut into the thing that keeps the boat in place.

**World facts (authored).**

| Field | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Atmosphere | breathable |
| Temperature | 4 °C |
| Radiation | normal |
| Biohazard | none (canister: "Salt, sand, fish oil. Nothing that grows on our plates.") |
| Life | people |
| Structures | settlement |
| True threat | LOW (good-confidence reason, from a survey or contact: "The village is not hostile. Its people fear visitors who come at the dark moon.") |
| Origin glyph | 13 (Fish) |
| Ring status | open, unguarded |

**Scene.** Biome `coast` (PO proposal: an authored-only value; if the scene enum cannot take
it, `steppe` with a sea horizon), sky low overcast with a pale sun, life people, structures
settlement, targets as below.

**MOTH texts.**

- **Look around:** "Shingle beach, cold sea, wind. A stone quay from the Ring to the water.
  Boats drawn up, nets drying. A long turf-roofed house with smoke. People: six to ten in view,
  human, in wool and oilskin; two have stopped work to watch the drone. Targets: the
  hearth-house, the boats, a carved post by the Ring." (Life people, Structures settlement,
  threat confidence fair.)
- **Close look, the hearth-house:** "Stone walls, turf roof, one door facing the sea. Above the
  door a hook of iron, the same make as the anchor on STILLWATER." (Photo.)
- **Close look, the boats:** "Clinker-built, about six metres, oars and one mast. Every boat
  carries an anchor; every anchor has a punched circle at its crown." (Photo.)
- **Close look, the carved post:** "A post of grey wood, as tall as a person. Notches run down
  it in groups, each group closed by a deep round hole. Thirty-odd notches between holes."
  (Photo. Not a puzzle: K3 explains it as a count of dark moons.)
- **Sky:** "Overcast; through a gap, two moons, one large, one small." (Photo.)
- **Ring:** the standard deeper console scan (1 to 2 older addresses).

None of the targets is recoverable.

**Horizon record and dossier** (on MOORING's first lock, as every world):

> WORLD DOSSIER: MOORING (PRELIMINARY)
> Source of address: marks on an iron anchor, STILLWATER (MOTH close look).
> Compiled from the horizon record of the first lock.
> Status: Inhabited. Do not send a team before contact is made.
>
> The far Ring stands at the end of a stone quay above a grey sea. The sampling line drew
> breathable air, cold and salt. Temperature four degrees. Wind off the water.
>
> The frames show a shingle beach, wooden boats drawn up above the tide, nets on frames, and a
> long low house with smoke from its roof. On the third frame a woman carrying a basket stops
> and looks straight at the camera. She does not run. She puts the basket down and walks
> toward the Ring.
>
> The pole camera was withdrawn on schedule. Whoever cut the way home into the anchor lives
> here, and now knows that someone has come through.
>
> Analyst's note: the boats are of a build that was common on our northern coasts a few
> thousand years ago. So are the faces.

## 3. Conversations K1 to K3

**Format.** Only the M3-15 node types: `line` (speaker, text key, minutes, next), `choice`
(each option: text key, conditions, effects, next), `check` (a skill check on the team leader;
pass and fail nodes) and `end` (effects). Conditions: `FlagIs`, `StandingAtLeast`, `WordKnown`.
Effects: `ChangeStanding`, `SetFlag`, `AddDocument`, `AddAddress`, `UnlockCodexEntry`. Word
tokens are written `{w:id}`; unknown ones render in script. Effects sit only on choices and
ends. Every choice is hidden when its conditions fail unless marked "show locked". Standing is
the Kindred's (`kindred`).

**Which conversation runs (PO proposal for cards 15 and 16).** A hail or a Contact arrival needs
to pick a conversation, and the format has no automatic branch. Each channel on MOORING gets an
ordered list of (conditions, conversation); the first whose conditions pass runs:

| Channel | Order |
| --- | --- |
| Hail | K1 if `FlagIs(kindred_met, false)`; K3 if `FlagIs(k3_done, false)` and `WordKnown(buried_ring)`; else K0 |
| Contact arrival | K2 if `FlagIs(k2_done, false)`; else K0 |

`kindred_met` is set by K1 or K2, whichever comes first; that is also when MOORING becomes
Contacted and First contact (+5 Confidence) is earned. A conversation cut off by "(signal
lost)" applies no end effects; `kindred_met` is set by the first choice of K1 and of K2, so a
first hail cut off after that choice still counts as contact. Hail is shown only
when life is "people" on the World Report.

**K0, the hearth is busy** (a fallback, 1 minute): line "The hearth-keeper is at the boats.
Come back when you have something new to say." → end (no effects).

      effects: ChangeStanding(kindred, -5), SetFlag(kindred_met), SetFlag(kindred_snubbed) -> e2
n3  line   oda  2  "Those who took us. If you stand against them, bring iron."  -> c2
n4  line   oda  2  "Only kin and {w:keepers} read hooks. Kin bring the hook home. {w:keepers} do not."  -> c2

the next choice: card 20 writes c2 twice, c2p after n5 (every option also sets

About 14 minutes of line time on the passed path. **Standing:** −5 to +15 (+5 or +10 opening,
+5 help; −5 for soldiers first). **Gives:** contact if it is the first, the oral history "The
taking" (long version on a pass, which reveals `collar` and `home_mark`), two Codex entries.
