# Playtest guide: M2.6

M2.6 adds people and politics: a staff you hire and assign, a Committee whose Confidence sets
your monthly money, and a month-end report that tells you why. This guide walks you through
your first month; the SILL answer is at the very end, behind a spoiler line.

Plan on 30 to 45 minutes, mostly at 10×. Keep notes as you play (see "What to report").

## What changed since M2.5

- **Corridors** paint with a mouse drag anywhere on the plan; the ghost no longer sticks at
  the bottom edge.
- Construction sites, demolitions and digs show **progress bars**.
- **Research** (F4) is now a tech tree with category sub-tabs. Pick a node, then **Start**.
- A new **Personnel** tab (F5): your staff, the monthly candidates, **Hire**, **Assign…**.
- **Quick save is now F8** (it was F5). Load stays F9.
- Staff matter: scientists set research speed, engineers speed up the **Workshop**, and the
  analyst in the Control Room files reports. **Quarters** add beds.
- **Committee Confidence** (0 to 100, "Conf." on the top bar) sets next month's allocation.
- At each month-end a **month-end report** pops up as a message. The game doesn't pause.

## 1. Start a new game

The game starts a **new game**, paused. Saves from earlier builds don't load: you get "This
save is from an older build and can't be loaded. Start a new game." Don't press Load (F9)
unless you saved (F8) in this build.

You start with five people: two scientists, two engineers and one analyst. Space resumes and
pauses; `=` and `-` change the speed.

## 2. Build the bank and the lab

In the Gate Room press **Build capacitor bank** and click once to build it. Then, in the
**BUILD** palette, pick **Corridor** and drag a short run off the long corridor. Pick
**Research Lab** and place it against your run. When the lab finishes, your two scientists
move in on their own: "Research Lab staffed: 2 scientists."

## 3. Lock SILL

When the bank holds enough, go to the Gate Room (F1), fill seals 1 to 7 and hold **Hold to
dial**. Close the wormhole when you're done. A first lock on a world earns Confidence.

## 4. Hire

Press F5 for **Personnel**. The header reads "Staff 5 · Beds 5/6". Open **Candidates (4)**,
pick Dr Ines Moreau (Scientist) and press **Hire (fee $30k)**. She shows on the **Roster** as
"Arriving in 5 d". Your sixth bed is now taken: hiring again needs **Quarters** (BUILD
palette, $150k, 4 beds).

## 5. Assign, and watch research speed up

When she arrives she is "Unassigned". Select her, press **Assign…** and pick "Research Lab
(Level 1): 2/4 · speed 100% to 150%". In **Research** (F4), pick **Excavation survey** and
press **Start**: its 2 days now take about 32 hours.

## 6. Paint another corridor

Back in the Base (F3), pick **Corridor** and drag an L-shaped run somewhere else on the plan,
just to feel the new drag. Watch the progress bars on anything you build or dig.

## 7. Let a month pass

Run at 10× to 1 February. A message appears, "Month-end report: Confidence 50 to 61" or
similar, and the game keeps running. Press **View** to read the report: allocation, upkeep,
salaries, balance, and each Confidence cause (a new lock, research finished, reports filed,
within budget). **Next allocation** is what you get on 1 March. **Close** (or Esc) shuts it;
hover **Conf.** on the top bar to see the same causes.

## What to report

Write down what you did, what you expected and what happened. The most useful notes:

1. **Hiring and staff.** Were Hire, Assign… and the beds clear? Did you understand what each
   person does, and see the research speed change?
2. **Confidence pacing.** Did +5, +3, −4 feel like they mattered? Would you play differently
   to earn more allocation?
3. **The month-end report.** Did you notice the message? Was the report easy to read, and was
   it right not to pause?
4. **The tech tree.** Easy to find projects, see what they unlock and why Start was greyed?
5. **Idle engineers.** Your two engineers wait, Unassigned, until you research and build the
   Workshop. Did that bother you, or make you want the Workshop sooner?
6. Any label, button or message that confused you. Quote it exactly. Anything that broke:
   note the in-game date on the top bar.
