# Playtest guide: M1.6

You run Site CAIRN, a base built around an alien Ring. You open wormholes by dialing addresses,
and you have to work every address out from the documents you are given. This build has four
story puzzles. This guide shows you the controls and nothing else: it gives no answers.

New in M1.6: you fill the console by clicking, the Gate Room shows the big Ring, an open
wormhole's actions come from clicking the portal, the pole camera is something you do, a lock
drops time to 1×, the Address Book is a tab on the right edge, and the Archive has a **Log**.

Plan on 30 to 60 minutes. Keep notes as you play (see "What to report" at the end).

## 1. Start a new game

Each time you launch the game, it starts a **new game**, paused. Saves from earlier builds
won't work with this one. **Don't press Load (F9) unless you saved in this build.** Save (F8; it was F5 before M2.6)
and Load (F9) on the top bar use one quick-save slot.

The top bar also holds the clock controls. **Resume** / **Pause** is Space or Start on a
gamepad. **Faster** and **Slower** are `=` and `-`, or the right and left triggers. Time only
passes while the clock runs. Most waiting goes fastest at the top speed (10×).

There are two tabs under the top bar: **Gate Room** (F1) and **Archive** (F2). Q and E, or the
gamepad shoulder buttons, switch between them. The game opens in the Gate Room.

## 2. The Gate Room

The Gate Room is the control room. The big **Ring** fills the view, as if through the glass.
The dial console sits below it, with the **Capacitors** gauge. On the right edge is a vertical
tab, **Address Book (…)**.

## 3. Install the capacitor bank

The Ring can't dial without stored energy, and the base starts with none. The **Capacitors**
gauge reads "No capacitors installed." Hold **Install capacitor bank (6 h)** until its bar
fills. Every costly button works this way: a quick press does nothing.

The install takes 6 in-game hours, and the clock has to run: press **Resume**. The gauge shows
"Installing: … left". Once the bank is in, the gauge shows its stored energy, "… of 400.0 MWh".
The base's plant charges it slowly.

## 4. Read the documents in the Archive

Press F2. The tab reads **Archive (2)**: the number counts the documents you have not read yet.
The Archive has two tabs, **Documents** and **Log**. A new game holds two documents, both marked
**New**. Choose one to read it. The reader shows its title, "Source: …", "Received …" and the
text. When a document has a picture, press it (or **View image**) to open it large, with
**Zoom in**, **Zoom out**, **Fit** and **Close**. **All kinds** filters the list by kind.

**Read two together:** with one document open, press **Read with…** and choose a second one.
The two open side by side. The second has its own **Close**.

**Log** lists what has happened at the base, newest first: dials, locks, closes, documents
received, consoles read, horizon records and power. **All events** filters it by kind, and
**Search the log** filters by text.

## 5. Enter an address on the dial console

Press F1. The console has eight seals in a row, with the 36-glyph palette below them.

- **Click a glyph to fill the next empty seal.** Seven clicks fill seals 1 to 7 in order. You
  can also drag a glyph onto a seal. On a gamepad, press A on a glyph.
- **Click a filled seal to remove its glyph.** The next glyph you click goes into that seal.
  Right-click, Delete or Backspace also clear a seal. **Clear all** empties all seven.
- **Seal 8 fills itself.** It is always CAIRN's own mark, and the palette shows that glyph
  locked. Never try to enter it.
- Placed glyphs are highlighted on the palette.

Once all seven are filled, hold **Hold to dial**. The gauge never shows how much energy an
address needs. If the bank is short, the Ring refuses ("Not enough charge. The Ring would not
turn."), spends nothing, and you can wait and try again. An address that has already failed
asks you to hold a second time, **Hold again to dial anyway**, because every dial spends energy.

## 6. The dialing sequence

The seals light one by one around the Ring ("Seals engaging: … of 8"). **Skip** (Escape,
gamepad B) jumps straight to the result, which is the same either way.

- **No gate**, **Blocked** or **Origin rejected**: a short card says what that tells you and
  how much energy the dial cost. Press **OK**. A wrong address costs a small part of the
  charge, not all of it.
- **Lock** or **Hostile lock**: the portal opens inside the Ring. **If time was running faster
  than 1×, it drops to 1×**, so the wormhole can't burn out unseen. You can speed up again.

## 7. The open portal

While a wormhole is open, a strip beside the Ring shows **Wormhole open**, the destination,
the time left, the power drain and the capacitors. When something arrived in the Archive, a
line names it: **New in the Archive: …**.

**Click the portal** (or press A with it focused) for **What you can do now**. Click it again,
or press B / Escape, to hide them.

- **Extend the pole camera** pushes a camera through the horizon. It takes a little time, so
  the clock has to run ("Horizon record in …"). When it is done, "Horizon record taken", and
  the record, plus any documents it brings, go to the Archive. The lock alone brings nothing:
  **extend the camera after every new lock.** The game pauses when documents arrive.
- **Read the far Ring's console** copies 2 to 4 nearby addresses into your Address Book,
  already written for CAIRN. Their worlds stay unknown until you lock them. Each console can
  be read once.
- **Read in the Archive** (only once documents arrived) opens the Archive on the first new
  one. The wormhole stays open; press F1 to come back to it.
- **Open the Address Book** opens the side panel.
- **Send a probe** stays disabled in this build.
- **Close the wormhole (hold)** closes it. A wormhole also closes by itself when its time or
  the charge runs out. The strip then says why; press **Dismiss**.

## 8. The Address Book

Press the **Address Book (…)** tab on the right edge to open it, and the tab again, B or
Escape to close it. It lists every address you have dialed and every address a console gave
you, newest first. Each row shows its source ("Dialed", "Console of …") and its world, or
"world unknown". Addresses from documents are not filled in for you: you enter those yourself.

- **Filters** shows the glyph grid, **Search notes and worlds** and the source menu (**All
  sources**, **Documents**, **Consoles**, **Dialed**). In the grid, press glyphs to show only
  the addresses that hold every one of them; press a glyph again to remove it. **Clear
  filters** resets them all.
- Select a row and press **Load** to put its address into the console; the panel closes.
  Type a note under the list and press **Save note** to keep it.

## What to report

Write down what you did, what you expected and what happened. The most useful notes:

1. **Does the Gate Room feel like a Stargate control room now?** The Ring, the seals lighting,
   the portal opening. What breaks the feeling?
2. **Is the portal interaction clear?** Did you think to click the portal? Did you find every
   action, and get back to them after the Archive or the Address Book?
3. **Is the pole camera a good beat?** Did you know you had to extend it after each lock? Did
   the wait feel right, and did you notice what it brought?
4. **Is the log useful?** Did you open **Log** in the Archive? Did it help you remember what
   you had tried? What was missing?
5. The four puzzles: where you got stuck, and for how long. Was the drop to 1× on a lock
   welcome or annoying?
6. Any label, button or message that confused you. Quote it exactly.
7. Anything that broke: errors, a stuck screen, time not moving, a dial refused that you
   expected to work. Note the in-game date shown on the top bar.

If you get stuck for more than 15 minutes, write down where, and ask for a hint.
