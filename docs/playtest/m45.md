# Playtest guide: M4.5, "Interface pass"

M4.5 changes how the game looks and how you move around it. It adds no new rules: research,
money, power, the gate and staff all work as before. Judge the look, the layout and the feel.
Plan on 30 to 45 minutes. Start a new game, or load an M4b save (saves are unchanged).

## What changed

- **Look.** The whole interface uses one theme: a dark grey-green ground, off-white text,
  orange for what is selected, green for on, amber for "soon", red for trouble. Blue is kept
  for the Ring. Labels use Barlow Condensed and numbers use IBM Plex Mono. Buttons look like
  keys and sink when pressed. Faint film grain lies over everything; turn it off with the
  `display/screen_grain` setting.
- **Top bar.** Left to right: clock and speed (with the call lamp), funds with today's net
  change (`+38/d`), Confidence with a trend arrow, power used over supply and stored energy,
  the gate chip, **Alerts**, then Save and Load. Each figure is a button: funds and Confidence
  open Command → Finances, power opens Base → Energy, the gate chip opens the Gate Room. In a
  narrow window the bar wraps onto a second row.
- **Gate chip.** IDLE (ring), DIALLING (half disc), INCOMING (amber triangle), or OPEN, which
  shows the world, the time left and a small live picture of the Ring.
- **Alerts.** The button shows how many alerts there are, with the worst one's lamp. The list
  is newest first. Choosing an alert opens its screen (a research alert also selects the
  project). Event alerts (research done, a team check-in, a hazard, a finished site) can be
  dismissed. Opening the list never pauses the game.
- **Tabs.** Tabs follow the loop, with icons: Gate Room F1, Base F2, Research F3, Personnel F4,
  Expeditions F5, Archive F6, Command F7. Q/E and the shoulders still cycle. A dot on a tab
  means something there needs you. If you rebound these keys earlier, your bindings are kept.
- **Base plan.** The plan is a flat schematic. A room that is on has a soft green glow. A room
  that is off is dim, with a dashed wall and the no-power mark. A site is hatched. A room
  coming down is hatched with a red cross. Each room has a type icon at top right. Furniture
  outlines appear from 1× zoom.
- **Staff on the plan.** Every person on base is a token. The shape and letter give the role:
  S circle, E square, A triangle. Unassigned people stand outside Quarters (or along the
  corridor) and are listed in the **Unassigned** strip under the plan. Hover a token for the
  name and role. Click a token to select the person and their room.
- **Walking.** When someone is reassigned, arrives or leaves, their token walks the corridors,
  using the lift between levels. Walking is faster at higher game speeds and stops while
  paused. A tab or level change puts everyone in place at once. The simulation has already
  moved the person; the walk is only for show.
- **Ambient life.** People shift about their room, sometimes visit another room, and at night
  most go to Quarters while one person stays in every room that is on. Turn this off with
  the `display/ambient_movement` setting.
- **Placing staff.** Drag a token onto a lit room to assign that person, or onto the
  Unassigned strip to unassign them. Rooms that can't take the person are dimmed. If the move
  is refused, a line says why. While dragging, hold the token over a level tab to switch
  level. Base has a new **Staff** sub-tab: it lists everyone on base and tints each room by
  how full it is. With a keyboard or gamepad, pick a person there, press **Assign**, move the
  cursor to a lit room and confirm. Esc or (B) cancels.
- **Camera inset.** Selecting a room shows its security camera at the top of the right column.
  The camera shows a camera number, the room name and the people in the room (they match the
  tokens). **Expand** grows the camera over the plan; Esc or (B) shrinks it first.
- **Gate Room.** The Ring is a 3D view through the control room glass. The chevrons light as
  the seals lock, and the room turns blue while a wormhole is open. The glyph marks, seal lamps
  and portal click are unchanged.
- **Layouts.** Research and Personnel use three columns: choose on the left, the board in the
  middle, detail and actions on the right. Command has **Finances**, **Factions** and
  **Requests** as sub-tabs on the left. The cork board is gone. A request alert opens Requests.

## What to try

1. Start a new game and read the top bar before touching anything. Is it clear what each
   figure is and where it leads?
2. Open **Alerts** and follow two of them. Dismiss a "finished building" alert after placing a
   room.
3. Go through every tab with F1 to F7, then with Q/E, then with a gamepad's shoulders.
4. On the Base, drag a scientist into the Research Lab and watch the walk. Drag them back to
   the Unassigned strip. Try a room that refuses them and read the reason.
5. Do the same with only the keyboard or gamepad: Base → Staff sub-tab → pick → Assign →
   confirm.
6. Leave the game running at each speed for a minute on the Base. Do people move enough, too
   much, or too little? Watch a night (22:00 to 06:00).
7. Select a few rooms and look at the camera. Expand it, then shrink it with Esc or (B).
8. Dial a known address. Watch the chevrons and the open Ring, then switch to the Base and
   look at the gate chip's thumbnail.
9. Try the game at 1280 × 720 in pt_BR, at 80% and at 150% UI scale.
10. Save, load, and check that tokens, alerts and the top bar come back right.

## Questions for the owner

1. Palette and grain: is the look right? Is the grain too strong, or should it be off by
   default?
2. Top bar: is anything missing or out of order? Should the funds show the 30-day change
   instead of the daily one?
3. Tab icons sit beside the labels rather than above them (we kept Godot's tab bar). Is that
   fine, or do you want the taller icon-over-label tabs?
4. Walking and ambient speed: is the pace right? Should ambient life be off by default?
5. Placing: drag, Assign, or both? Should a tray entry be draggable as well as clickable?
6. Camera inset: is a 320 × 240 inset the right size? Should the feed refresh more slowly to
   save performance? On software rendering one feed costs about 9 ms per frame.
7. Research: the category tabs stayed above the tree because seven tabs don't fit a 240 px
   column. Do you want them as a list on the left instead?
8. Is there any screen where text is cut off or overlaps, in English or pt_BR?

## Known limits

- "Finished building" alerts and dismissals last only for the session; they are gone after a
  load.
- The ambient movement and grain switches exist as settings but there is no settings screen
  for them yet.
- "Off" and "no power" look the same on the plan; the simulation doesn't tell them apart.
