# Playtest guide: M4.10, "Frame and the wire"

The game now has a frame bolted round every station: a clock plate and gauges at the top,
the wire of event cards down the left, four station keys bottom left and a gate monitor
bottom right. The old top bar, the alerts drop-down and the log line along the bottom are
gone. Also in this build: the M4.9 follow-ups (a mid-game reload fix, the alien panel's
layout, the clue clip, the event horizon, people in the gate hall). Plan on 20 minutes.
Saves from earlier builds may not load; start a new game or a scenario.

Screenshots at 1920 × 1080 (`final_01_gate_frame.png` to `final_08_alien_panel.png`: the
Gate with the frame, the Base with the wire, the Office, an offer in a conversation, the gate
monitor during a dial, the open horizon, the opening burst, the alien panel) were handed to
the owner with this build; they are not in the repository.

## Start here

Main menu > Scenarios > **Gate station**. A phone call from the Committee waits, four
candidates are on offer and two worlds are known. The clock starts paused.

## What changed

- **The clock plate** (top left): the date, the speed, and three keys: **pause**, **play**
  (resumes; while running, one speed slower) and **fast**. The SLOW lamp and the call lamp
  (the caller's name) sit beside them; hover for the reason. Space and the shoulders work as
  before.
- **The gauges** (top right): funds with today's change, power used over supply, capacitor
  charge and Confidence, one needle and one number each. Hover for what it is; click to open
  the place that owns it.
- **The wire** (left): one card per thing that needs you, newest first, with its own keys:
  a candidate (**hire**, pass), the lab idle or research done (the three cheapest projects
  you can start, and the tree), a request offered (**accept**, decline), an artefact at the
  gate (house, study), a room finished (switch on, assign), a team overdue (rescue), an
  incoming wormhole or a phone call (**answer**, ignore), funds or charge low (go there), a
  team home (report). A greyed key says why on hover. At most five cards show; the stack
  under them counts the rest and opens the full log. Cards with an **×** can be dismissed;
  the others go when their cause does. The wire never pauses the game.
- **Station keys** (bottom left): **Gate** (F1), **Base** (F2, with Personnel as its second
  folder), **Lab** (F3, once a Research Lab exists) and **Office** (F4: Command, Archive and
  Personnel). Q and E cycle them. The tabs above a station are now its folders. A lamp on a
  key means something there needs you.
- **The gate monitor** (bottom right, not on the Gate): a small screen that shows the Ring
  live while it dials, is open or something comes through; IDLE otherwise. Click it to go to
  the Gate.
- **Requests in conversations.** When someone offers a request, the answers are **Accept**,
  **Decline** and **I will think about it**, with the terms on a paper card beside the talk.
  Thinking about it leaves it on the wire.
- **Save and Load** are in the pause menu (Esc) only; F5 and F8 quick save and load as before.
- **The Gate** (M4.9 follow-ups): the alien panel fits every screen size and its clue is a
  paper document on a clipboard; the mission sheet closes with the wormhole, Esc or the alien
  panel; the event horizon is watery with a burst when it opens; technicians stand at the
  consoles and a soldier at the blast door, and someone crosses the floor now and then.

## Try this

1. On the Base (F2), press **hire** on the candidate card. Watch the card change.
2. Press **answer** on the phone call card. Read the briefing; at the offer, look at the
   paper card, then **Accept**. The request is now under Office > Command > Requests.
3. Build a Research Lab, let it finish and switch it on: a LAB IDLE card offers projects.
   Start one from the card.
4. From the Office (F4), go back to the Gate (F1), pick SILL's card and DIAL, then press F4
   quickly: the gate monitor shows the seals lighting.
5. Open the Gate and press **+**: the alien panel should fit the screen with nothing
   overlapping. Step the clue with the arrows.
6. Watch the hall for a minute with the clock running: people at the consoles, a walk across
   the floor. Dial, and watch the horizon open.

## Known gaps

- On the old screens (Base, Personnel, Archive, Command, Research) the screen starts right of
  the wire, so they are narrower; on the Gate the wire lies over the scene. Phases 3 and 4
  redraw those screens.
- The tab bar is still there, as the current station's folders; on the Gate and the Lab it
  has one tab.
- **pass** (candidate) and **ignore** (phone) only dismiss the card: there is no way to turn a
  candidate away, and the call stays on the call lamp.
- The event horizon costs about 4 to 6 ms a frame on a software renderer; it could not be
  measured on a real integrated graphics card here.
- The alien panel's placeholder plate still draws its old ring outline behind the keys.

## Questions for you

1. **The wire.** Is the left edge the right place? Are five cards too many or too few? Which
   card did you want a key on that it does not have?
2. **The gauges.** Can you read the four needles at a glance, or do you read the numbers?
3. **The station keys.** Do Gate, Base, Lab and Office feel like places? Is bottom left
   right, or should they sit in one bottom bar with the gate monitor?
4. **Accepting in the conversation.** Is the paper card enough to decide, or do you miss the
   Command screen's detail?
5. **The horizon and the hall.** Does the opening burst read as the Ring opening? Are the
   people in the hall too few, too many, too busy?
6. What text is still too long or unclear anywhere on the frame or the Gate?
