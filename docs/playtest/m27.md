# Playtest guide: M2.7

M2.7 moves everything monthly to a daily rhythm: money is paid and charged every day,
Confidence rises the moment something happens and then slowly settles, a new candidate turns
up every 3 days, and a **Command** tab (F6) shows your finances and Confidence at all times.
This guide walks you through your first week or so; the SILL answer is at the very end,
behind a spoiler line.

Plan on 30 to 45 minutes, mostly at 10×. Keep notes as you play (see "What to report").

## What changed since M2.6

- **Quarters** is in the Base tab's **BUILD** palette ($150k, 4 beds).
- A room you're placing is easy to put away: right-click, Esc or (B), the same palette entry
  again, the new **Cancel** button next to Build, or switching tab or level. The prompt says so.
- The Base tab has two sub-tabs, **Build** and **Energy** (P switches; the shoulders step
  through them). Energy is the old power overlay.
- **Digging** is now a construction on the Base tab: **Dig Level 2** sits in the Build
  palette, and the level tabs show "L2 0%" while it digs.
- **Money is daily.** At 00:00 each day (from 2 January) you get $(50 + Confidence)k and pay
  a thirtieth of your upkeep and salaries. There is no month-end report any more.
- **Confidence moves daily.** A lock (+5) or a finished project (+3) counts at once, then
  Confidence drifts 0.1 a day back toward 50 (−0.2 a day while your balance is below zero).
  When it reaches 70 the Committee pays a $1,000k commendation (again only after it has fallen below 60).
- A new **Command** tab (F6): funds, the money lines per day and per 30 days, the trend,
  recent causes, warnings and the Committee. Clicking **Conf.** or the funds on the top bar
  opens it.
- **Candidates every 3 days.** One arrives on 4 January, 7 January and so on; each stays
  12 days ("Leaves in 5 d").

## 1. Start a new game

The game starts a **new game**, paused. Saves from earlier builds don't load: you get "This
save is from an older build and can't be loaded. Start a new game." Don't press Load (F9)
unless you saved (F8) in this build.

Space resumes and pauses; `=` and `-` change the speed.

## 2. Try Quarters, then cancel it

Press F3 for the Base tab; it opens on **Build**. In the **BUILD** palette pick
**Quarters**: its entry stays pressed and a ghost follows the mouse. Now right-click on the
plan. The ghost goes away and nothing is spent. Try the other ways out too: Esc, **Cancel**,
or **Quarters** again.

## 3. Build the bank and the lab, then start the survey

In the Gate Room (F1) press **Build capacitor bank** and click once to build it. Back in the
Base, pick **Corridor** and drag a short run off the long corridor, then pick **Research
Lab** and place it against your run. When the lab finishes, press F4 for **Research**, pick
**Excavation survey** and press **Start**.

## 4. Lock SILL and watch the day turn

When the bank holds enough, go to the Gate Room (F1), fill seals 1 to 7 and hold **Hold to
dial**. Close the wormhole when you're done. The top bar's **Conf.** goes from "Conf. 50" to
"Conf. 55 -0.1" at once: the -0.1 is today's drift back toward 50. Let the clock pass
midnight: your funds go up by the day's allocation less the day's costs, and Conf. reads
"Conf. 54 -0.1".

## 5. Open Command

Click **Conf.** (or press F6). The **Command** tab shows "Funds … (+… a day)", the
**MONEY** lines with **per day** and **per 30 days**, the **TREND**, **RECENT CAUSES** (your
SILL lock and, once it finishes, Excavation survey) and **WARNINGS**. The **COMMITTEE** panel
on the right shows Confidence with one decimal, today's drift and why, and the
**Commendation** bar. Click a money line to see its breakdown; (B) closes it.

## 6. Hire the 4 January arrival

Run to 4 January. Press F5 for **Personnel** and open **Candidates**: a new candidate has
arrived, and Dr Ines Moreau has left. Each row shows "Leaves in … d". Pick the new one and
press **Hire (fee …)**. They show on the **Roster** as "Arriving in …". Your sixth bed is now
taken: the next hire needs **Quarters**.

## 7. Dig Level 2

When Excavation survey is done, go back to the Base (F3), **Build** sub-tab. Hold **Dig
Level 2: $1,500k, 3 d**. The level tabs show "L2 0%" climbing; when it reaches the end,
**L2** opens an empty level. Press P to look at **Energy**, and P again to come back.

## 8. Let a week or two pass

Run at 10×. Watch the funds tick up each midnight, Conf. drift, and the Candidates count
change every 3 days. On 15 January your analyst files reports (+1). Check Command now and
then.

## What to report

Write down what you did, what you expected and what happened. The most useful notes:

1. **Daily money and Confidence pacing.** Did a daily payday feel better than the monthly
   one? Did +5 and +3, then the slow drift back to 50, feel like they mattered? Did you
   ever chase the 70 commendation?
2. **The Command tab.** Did you use it? Were the money lines, trend and causes easy to read,
   and was anything missing that the old month-end report had?
3. **Candidates every 3 days.** Was the pace right? Did "Leaves in" make you hire sooner, or
   did you miss someone you wanted?
4. **Base sub-tabs and digging.** Were Build and Energy easy to find and switch? Was it clear
   where to dig, and what the "L2 0%" tab meant?
5. **Placement cancel.** Did right-click, Esc, Cancel or re-picking the entry do what you
   expected? Did you ever cancel by accident?
6. Any label, button or message that confused you. Quote it exactly. Anything that broke:
   note the in-game date on the top bar.
