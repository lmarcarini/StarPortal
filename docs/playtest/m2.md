# Playtest guide: M2

New in M2: the **Base** tab. You build rooms on a top-down plan of Site CAIRN, share the
plant's power between them, dig new levels and watch the money. The Ring now runs on a
Capacitor Bank you build yourself. This guide shows you the controls and gives no puzzle
answers.

Plan on 30 to 45 minutes. Keep notes as you play (see "What to report" at the end).

## 1. Start a new game

The game starts a **new game**, paused. Saves from earlier builds don't load in this one: you
get "This save is from an older build and can't be loaded. Start a new game." Don't press Load
(F9) unless you saved in this build.

**Resume** / **Pause** is Space (Start on a gamepad). `=` and `-` (the triggers) change the
speed. Time only passes while the clock runs.

There are three tabs: **Gate Room** (F1), **Archive** (F2) and **Base** (F3). Q and E, or the
gamepad shoulder buttons, also switch tabs. The money shows on the top bar.

## 2. Build the capacitor bank from the Gate Room

The game opens in the Gate Room. The Capacitors gauge reads "No capacitors installed." Press
**Build capacitor bank**. The game switches to the Base tab on Level 3, with the Capacitor
Bank's outline already placed on the free spot nearest the Gate Room (nothing on the plan is
reserved for it). Click it on the plan (or press
**Build**, Enter or (A)) to build. It costs $600k and takes 6 hours.

## 3. Watch it charge

Press **Resume**. The site shows its time left. When the bank is built, the game pauses and
the spare power goes to charging it: the power bar at the top of the Base tab shows a
**Charge** block, and beside it "Capacitors …/400 MWh, full in … h". The first lock takes
about 14 hours of charging. Back in the Gate Room (F1), the gauge fills too.

## 4. Lock SILL and send the pole camera

Dial SILL as in M1.6: fill seals 1 to 7 on the console, then hold **Hold to dial**. When the
wormhole opens, click the portal for **What you can do now**, choose **Extend the pole
camera** and let the clock run until the record comes back. Then **Close the wormhole
(hold)**.

## 5. Build a lab with a corridor

Press F3. Use PageUp and PageDown (Y and X on a gamepad) to change level. In the **BUILD**
palette at the bottom, pick **Corridor** and drag a run of cells from an existing corridor.
Then pick **Physics Lab** and move its outline so one edge touches your corridor. Q or E
rotates it. A red outline says why it can't go there ("Must touch a corridor.", "Overlaps the
…", "Needs …; you have …."). Click to build; the outline stays out to place another. Esc, (B)
or a right click leaves placing.

Click a room on the plan to see it in the **SELECTED** panel: draw, upkeep, status and
**Demolish (hold)**. A site shows **Cancel (hold)** and its refund. With nothing to cancel,
Esc or (B) on the plan moves focus to the **BUILD** palette.

## 6. Use the energy overlay

Press P, **Energy (P)** or the View button on a gamepad. Each room shows its switch: `ON 4`,
`OFF`, `LOCK 2` for rooms that are always on (their draw is shown), `+30` for the plant and `− CHG 26 +` for the
bank.

- **Switch rooms.** Your new lab finishes **off**, because the bank takes all the spare power.
  Click it: the game refuses with "Needs 4 MW; 0 MW spare."
- **Change the charge rate.** Press `−` on the bank (or select it and use its **Charge rate**
  control) to free a few MW. Now switch the lab on. Charging is slower, and the "full in" time
  on the bar grows.

An open wormhole drains the capacitors at 20 MWh per hour while charging continues. At the
starting 26 MW the bank keeps up; below 20 MW an open wormhole can run the capacitors dry and
close.

## 7. Dig a level

In the palette, hold **Dig Level 4** ($3,000k, 20 days). The level column on the left shows
**L4** with the days left. When it is dug, it is an empty level you can build on.

## 8. Let a month pass

Run the clock to the 1st of the next month (10× helps). At midnight the base receives its
$3,000k allocation and pays every room's upkeep; a room that is off pays half. Check the money
on the top bar before and after.

## What to report

Write down what you did, what you expected and what happened. The most useful notes:

1. **Did the Base tab make sense without help?** Finding the palette, placing a room, the red
   reasons, clicking to build.
2. **Is the energy overlay clear?** Did you understand why the lab was off, and how to free
   power for it? Is the charge rate worth managing, or a chore?
3. **The open wormhole now drains while charging continues.** Did a wormhole ever close on
   you for lack of charge? Did that feel fair? Was the 20 MWh/h drain too weak or too strong?
4. **Money.** Did you ever run short? Did the month-end change feel right?
5. **Does the plan look like a lit underground base?** What breaks the feeling?
6. Any label, button or message that confused you. Quote it exactly.
7. Anything that broke: errors, a stuck screen, a refused action you expected to work. Note
   the in-game date shown on the top bar.
