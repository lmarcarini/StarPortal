# Playtest guide: M4.9, "Gate station"

The Gate tab is now one place in the base: the control room window onto the Ring, with a
console along the bottom. The old Gate Room, the Address Book drawer, the glyph palette and
the Expeditions tab are gone. Plan on 20 to 30 minutes. **Saves from earlier builds do not
load** (save format 26, no migration, owner decision); start a new game or a scenario.

## Start here

Main menu > Scenarios > **Gate station**. Two worlds are known (SILL and LINTEL), the
Capacitor Bank is full, Expeditions are unlocked with a team ready, and the MOTH rack holds two
MOTHs. The clock starts paused.

## What changed

- **The plate.** The Ring at the end of its ramp, with the floor, the blast door and people on
  it. The Ring's layers are live: seals light while dialling, the event horizon fills it, the
  Shutter closes over it, and people walk up and down the ramp.
- **The console.** Left: the destination monitor (the world's picture and status icons).
  Centre: seven seal lamps over one big key: **DIAL**, **CLOSE** with a wormhole open
  (held when closing would cut a MOTH or canister short), **ANSWER** with an **X** key beside
  it when an incoming wormhole waits. Right: the charge needle and, once installed, the Shutter
  lever. A refusal shakes the key; its reason is on the key's hover card.
- **Destination cards** on the right wall replace the Address Book. Click a card to put it on
  the monitor, then DIAL. Right click (F, right stick) flips it to the World Report, with your
  note on the address below it. U (Share) pins it to the top. Pins are saved. The filter key
  on top cycles all, with a team, never reached. **+ NEW ADDRESS** at the foot opens the alien
  panel.
- **The alien panel.** 36 stone keys in three rings round a centre DIAL key, the seal lamps
  across the top, a pencil for guesses and a clue clip with one document. Typing a glyph's
  name finds it. What you entered and your pencilled guesses are kept in the save.
- **With a wormhole open: the send rail** on the floor right of the ramp. CAMERA, CANISTER,
  MOTH, TEAM, HAIL (only when someone can answer) and the far CONSOLE (reads the far Ring's
  console log into new cards), keys 1 to 5. A small clock key stands by (keeps the wormhole
  open, stops slowing time).
- **Locker**, high on the left: a crate (the artefacts held, opens the list) and the MOTH order
  key (owned of two).
- **Teams.** TEAM on the rail opens the mission sheet in place: mission icons, the team as four
  face tiles (tap one to swap), GO. A card's TEAM key does a **mission order** from a closed
  gate: the base dials, the team goes through, the gate closes. The team card low on the left
  shows the time left as a ring.
- **Talking to a team** needs an open gate. HAIL opens the dialogue screen with the team leader;
  the answers are the orders (Carry on, Come home, Stay longer). A team in trouble dials in; ANSWER
  and the same screen gives Come through or Hold on.
- **The team report.** When a team comes home (or is rescued or lost) a full-screen report
  opens and holds the clock: the outcome, the world, the mission, days out, the team as face
  tiles with injuries, what came back, and keys for the World Report and the holding list.
  Pause menu > "Open the team report on return" turns that off; the report then waits on the
  team card. A team's return in Archive > Log reopens its report.

## Try this

1. Click SILL's card, press DIAL. Watch the seals and the horizon.
2. CAMERA, then MOTH: run a scan and recall it. Throw the canister if you like.
3. TEAM, check the sheet, GO. Watch them walk up the ramp. CLOSE.
4. A day later dial SILL again, HAIL the team, answer Come home. Close the report when it opens.
5. From a closed gate, press the TEAM key on LINTEL's card and GO.
6. + NEW ADDRESS: pencil a guess, close the panel, save, load: the guess is still there.

## Known gaps

- **No duration dial** on the mission sheet: a mission lasts its default days.
- **No "Bring home" key on the team card**: call a team home by hailing it through an open gate.
- **No "need" mark on the charge gauge**: the research that would unlock it does not exist yet;
  the owner will redo the research tree later.
- The gate chip in the top bar no longer shows a live picture of the Ring (the gate monitor
  comes in phase 2).
- The station plates are placeholders until the owner supplies the final art.

## Questions for you

1. **The plate and its lighting.** Does the room read as a control room looking down at the
   Ring? Is it too dark or too bright? Do the lit seals and the horizon carry the moment?
2. **The size of the console.** Is the console too tall or too short for the plate? Is DIAL
   big enough? Can you read the charge needle at a glance?
3. **The alien panel's key layout.** Do the three rings of twelve help you learn where a glyph
   is? Is the centre key the obvious place to dial? Is the clue clip big enough to read?
4. **Text.** What still needs words to be understood? Which hover cards did you have to read,
   and which icon did you not recognise?
5. **The next plate.** What should the next version of the plate fix first: the ramp, the
   floor space for people, the rail's place, the right wall for the cards?
6. Did the team report and the team conversations make it clear what happened out there?
