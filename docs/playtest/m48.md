# Playtest guide: M4.8, "Dialogue screen"

Conversations now take the whole screen and stop the game. A window on the left shows whoever
is talking through a video link. The portraits are **placeholders** drawn in code (a simple
head and shoulders); real portraits and personalities are the next conversation with the owner.
Plan on 15 to 20 minutes. Saves from M4.7 and M4c load (they migrate to format 22).

## What changed

- **Full-screen and modal.** Answering a call, an incoming wormhole or hailing a world opens
  the dialogue screen over everything. The tabs, the top bar and the base cannot be reached
  until you close it. Esc (Start) still opens the pause menu, so you can save or quit.
- **Time stops.** No tick runs from the moment the conversation opens until you close it. No
  money, no drain on the open wormhole, no capacitor charge used, nothing happens at the base.
  The top strip says **BASE ON HOLD** and shows the stopped date. When you close, the clock
  goes back to the speed and the pause state it had.
- **You move it on.** A line types out at about 40 characters a second. **Space, Enter, a
  click on the text or (A)** shows the whole line; pressed again it goes to the next line.
  Nothing times out.
- **Answers.** Up to four large keys numbered 1 to 4 (keys 1 to 4, a click, or D-pad and (A)).
  A locked answer is greyed with its reason. Your answers appear in the transcript as **YOU**.
  Earlier lines stay above, dimmed; Up and Down, the wheel or the right stick scroll them.
- **The portrait window.** Every channel uses the same video look (scanlines, grain, a slow
  roll). The frame is **Ring blue** for the Ring and **CRT green** for the Earth secure line,
  with its label. A moving portrait reads LIVE; some callers are a **STILL** (the Committee
  Liaison), a fixed picture with no breathing, blinking or mouth.
- **Placeholder figures.** They breathe and blink, move the mouth while the line types, and
  show a mood when a line has one: pleased, wary or angry (brows, posture and the colour of the
  light). Try the first Kindred hail (answer each way) and the Ossar demand.
- **Name plate** under the portrait: name in the faction's colour, title, and the standing (or
  Confidence for the Committee).
- **Opening and closing.** About a second of static with ESTABLISHING LINK (or CONNECTING
  SECURE LINE) while the screen fades in; Continue skips it. At the end the picture cuts to
  LINK CLOSED (or SIGNAL LOST) and only **Close** is offered. There is no summary: what changed
  is in the Log and on the tabs, and the transcript is in the Archive as before.
- **The wormhole cannot be closed** while a conversation runs over it, and a conversation is no
  longer cut off by the charge running out.
- **Reduced motion** (`display/ambient_movement` off) keeps the portraits still except the
  mouth and skips the opening.

## What to try

1. Set 10×. Dial HEARTH and hail the Kindred. Watch the opening, read the lines with Space,
   answer, and close. The clock should be back at 10× and the date unchanged.
2. Hail with the game paused: closing should leave it paused.
3. Answer the Committee's call (day 2). Note the green frame and the STILL picture.
4. Mid-conversation, press Esc, save, then load that save: the screen should come back at the
   same line.
5. Try the whole conversation with a gamepad only, then with the keyboard only.
6. Switch the language to Português and check that long answers fit.

## Questions for the owner

1. **Typing speed.** Is 40 characters a second right, or should it be faster, slower, or a
   setting?
2. **Text size.** The current line is 20 px and earlier lines 16 px (at UI scale 100%). Big
   enough to read from the sofa? Too big?
3. **Still versus animated.** The Liaison is a still. Which callers should be stills (the
   Committee? anyone on the phone?) and which should move?
4. **What should portraits become?** Layered 2D art with a few expressions, a 3D head through
   the camera kit, painted stills, or something else? Should aliens and humans be drawn the same
   way? This shapes the follow-up on portraits and personalities.
5. Is the opening (about a second) worth keeping, or would you rather go straight to the first
   line?

## What to report

Any key or button that reaches the game behind the screen, any time the clock moved during a
conversation, any line or answer that does not fit, and anything you cannot do with a gamepad.
