# Playtest guide: M3a, "The first Threshold team"

M3a takes you past the Ring for the first time: you probe a world with the sample canister
and the MOTH drone, read what you learned in the **World Report**, plan a team of four on the
new **Expeditions** tab (F7), send them through, follow their check-ins and bring them home,
or rescue them when a check-in doesn't come. This guide walks you through your first team;
the SILL answer is at the very end, behind a spoiler line.

Plan on 45 to 60 minutes, mostly at 10×. Keep notes as you play (see "What to report").
M3b (MOORING and contact) is held until after this playtest, so your notes shape it.

## What changed since M2.7

- **Ring actions** on an open wormhole now include **Throw the sample canister ($10k)**
  (biohazard in 10 min) and **Send the MOTH** ("2 in the rack").
- The **MOTH scan screen** opens over the Gate Room: **Look around**, **Close look** at each
  target, **Sky** and **Ring**, each costing battery minutes (20 in all). **RECALL** brings
  the drone home; on dangerous worlds it can be brought down. A lost drone is replaced in a
  day for $150k.
- The Archive has a **Worlds** section: one **World Report** per world you've reached, with
  its state (LOCKED, PROBED, SURVEYED), threat and confidence, facts, targets and
  **Plan expedition**.
- **Expeditions unlock** when your first world is Probed: the **Expeditions** tab (F7)
  appears, the Directorate lends Maj. Ruth Adair and Sgt Owen Hale (soldiers, no beds), and
  the **Infirmary** ($300k, 4 patients) joins the Base tab's **BUILD** palette.
- **Soldiers and medics** now turn up among the candidates.
- Teams **check in every 24 hours** by themselves; a missed check-in opens a **rescue alert**
  with a 72-hour window. Closing a wormhole with a probe out now warns you first.
- The **Prologue ends** when your first team comes home, with a message and a Log line.

## 1. Start a new game

The game starts a **new game**, paused. Saves from earlier builds don't load: you get "This
save is from an older build and can't be loaded. Start a new game." Don't press Load (F9)
unless you saved (F8) in this build.

Space resumes and pauses; `=` and `-` change the speed.

## 2. Lock SILL and look

In the Gate Room (F1) press **Build capacitor bank** and click once to build it. Run at 10×
until the bank holds enough, fill seals 1 to 7 with SILL's address and hold **Hold to dial**.
The clock drops to 1× while the wormhole is open. Click the Ring for its actions ("What you
can do now") and press **Extend the pole camera**.

## 3. Probe it

Still on the open wormhole, press **Throw the sample canister ($10k)**: "Soil and water tested
for biohazard in 10 min." Then press **Send the MOTH**. The scan screen opens: run **Look
around**, then **Close look: Flat slab before the Ring**, then **Sky**. Watch the **BATTERY**
and "Recall needs 2 min", and press **RECALL** before it runs out. Keep an eye on the
wormhole's time left; if you close it with the canister or the MOTH still out, it warns you
("Closing now loses …") and you hold again to close anyway.

When the MOTH is back, SILL is **PROBED**. The Log says "The Directorate lends Maj. Ruth Adair
(Soldier, skill 3)." and the same for Sgt Owen Hale, and **Expeditions** appears among the
tabs.

## 4. Read the World Report

Press F2 for the **Archive** and open **Worlds**. Pick **SILL**: the report shows its state,
**Threat** with its confidence and source, the facts you've learned (the canister's
biohazard, the MOTH's life and structures), and **Targets**. Press **Plan expedition**.

## 5. Plan the team

**Plan expedition** opens the **Expeditions** tab (F7) with SILL chosen. Under **Objective**,
**Survey** is picked (**Recover** shows on worlds with something to bring back). Fill the four
**Team** slots: **Leader** (anyone), **Science**, **Soldier** and **Special**. Each list shows
skill and what the base loses while that person is away. Read the **Risk** line, then press
**Ready team**: "Team ready: Send team from the Ring when that world is open."

## 6. Send them

Go back to the Gate Room (F1). If the wormhole is still open, the Ring actions now show
**Send team** ("T-1 to SILL: Survey, 2 days."). Otherwise dial SILL again. Press **Send
team**: T-1 goes through at once. Then hold **Close the wormhole (hold)**.

## 7. Wait for the check-ins

Run at 10×. After 24 hours a message says "T-1 checked in from SILL …" with any injuries;
the game doesn't pause. On the Expeditions tab, the middle shows the timeline ("Next
check-in …", **Recall team**) and the right the latest **CHECK-IN**. Try **Recall team** on a
later trip if you want to see it. At 48 hours the team comes home, SILL becomes **SURVEYED**,
and **PAST EXPEDITIONS** lists "Survey report from SILL". Your first team home ends the
Prologue: a message and a Log line say "Prologue complete: the first Threshold team is home."

## 8. Heal and hire

If anyone came back hurt, build an **Infirmary** from the Base tab's **BUILD** palette (F3)
and staff it with a medic from the candidates (F5, **Candidates**). Injured people show
"injured" in the team lists and can't be sent until they heal.

## 9. Keep sending teams

Plan and send more Surveys, on SILL or on other worlds you reach. Sooner or later a check-in
won't come: the game pauses and the rescue alert opens, "T-1 MISSED ITS CHECK-IN (…)", with
the **Window**, what to do, the threat and the charge forecast. If it says the bank won't be
ready in time, press **Charge at maximum**. Press **Go to Gate Room**, dial the team's world
and press **Signal T-1** on the Ring: they reach the Ring 15 minutes later, so keep the
wormhole open. **Close** hides the alert; "T-1 missing …" on the top bar brings it back.
Not everyone may come back, and waiting lowers the odds.

## What to report

Write down what you did, what you expected and what happened. The most useful notes:

1. **Probing and the MOTH screen.** Did the canister and the MOTH feel worth the time on an
   open wormhole? Was the battery and "Recall needs" clear? Did you ever lose a drone, and did
   it feel fair?
2. **The World Report.** Did you read it before planning? Were the threat, confidence and
   targets useful, and was anything missing?
3. **Planning and the risk shown.** Was the plan sheet easy to fill? Did the **Risk** line
   and "what the base loses" change whom you sent?
4. **Check-in rhythm.** Did a check-in every 24 hours feel right at 10×? Did you notice the
   messages, and use **Recall team**?
5. **The rescue.** Was the alert tense in a good way? Was the window and the charge forecast
   clear, and did the outcome feel fair (who survived, and why)?
6. **Field events.** Did the findings and setbacks in the check-ins and the Log read well?
   Did they feel tied to the world and the team?
7. **Soldiers, medics and the Infirmary.** Did the lent soldiers help? Did you hire soldiers
   or medics, and was the Infirmary worth building?
8. **The Expeditions tab.** Were the plan, timeline, latest check-in and past expeditions
   easy to read and find?
9. Any label, button or message that confused you. Quote it exactly. Anything that broke:
   note the in-game date on the top bar.
