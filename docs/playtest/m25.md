# Playtest guide: M2.5

M2.5 answers your M2 notes: a leaner start, much faster building, research, and power in
5 MW blocks. This guide walks you through the new opening and the research path; the SILL
answer is at the very end, behind a spoiler line.

Plan on 30 to 45 minutes, mostly at 10×. Keep notes as you play (see "What to report").

## What changed since M2

- You start with one level: the Gate Room, Control Room, one diesel plant and $6,000k.
- Building takes one click, and every build is 6 to 24 times faster.
- A **Research Lab** replaces the Physics Lab. Research unlocks digging, **Stairs**, **Lift**,
  more plants, cheaper fuel, more banks and the **Workshop**.
- Research has its own tab, **Research** (F4), shown once a Research Lab is built.
- Power comes in 5 MW blocks. A room that finishes switches on by lowering charging first.
- Each room says what it does now, and what it will do later.
- The Archive room, Quarters and Infirmary are gone for now.

## 1. Start a new game

The game starts a **new game**, paused. Saves from earlier builds don't load: you get "This
save is from an older build and can't be loaded. Start a new game." Don't press Load (F9)
unless you saved in this build.

Space resumes and pauses; `=` and `-` change the speed.

## 2. Build the capacitor bank

The game opens in the Gate Room, with "No capacitors installed". Press **Build capacitor
bank**. The Base tab opens with the bank's outline next to the Gate Room: click once to build
it ($600k, 1 hour).

## 3. Charge

Press Space. When the bank is built, the spare 25 MW charges it: SILL needs about 15 hours.

## 4. Build the Research Lab

Right away, in the **BUILD** palette, pick **Corridor** and drag a short run off the long
corridor. Then pick **Research Lab** and place it against your run ($800k, 8 hours). When it
finishes it switches on and takes its 5 MW from charging; you see "Research Lab on: charging
lowered to 20 MW." SILL is now ready about 17 hours in.

## 5. Start research

Press F4 (or the **Research** tab). Pick **Excavation survey** and press **Start** ($400k,
2 days). One project runs at a time; the header shows the time left.

## 6. Dial SILL

Back in the Gate Room (F1), fill seals 1 to 7 and hold **Hold to dial**. Send the pole camera
if you like, then **Close the wormhole (hold)**.

## 7. Dig Level 2 and place stairs down

When the survey is done, hold **Dig Level 2** in the palette ($1,500k, 3 days). When it is
dug, pick **Stairs** on Level 1 and place them against a corridor: they reach down to Level 2,
where new corridors can join them.

## 8. Lift, Workshop, second bank

In **Research**, run **Lift shaft**, then build a **Lift** (it takes the same 2 × 2 cells on
every level). Run **Field engineering** and build a **Workshop**: while it is on, sites and
digs finish 25% faster. Run **Bank array** and build a second **Capacitor Bank**. Money runs
short around here; the month-end allocation tops it up.

## What to report

Write down what you did, what you expected and what happened. The most useful notes:

1. **Build times and pacing.** Too fast, too slow, or right? Did you spend long stretches at
   10× with nothing to do, or feel rushed?
2. **Is research clear?** Did you find the Research tab, understand what each project
   unlocks and why Start was greyed when it was?
3. **The 5 MW blocks.** Are the power numbers easier to read? Did charging being lowered when
   a room finished make sense, and did you notice the message?
4. Any label, button or message that confused you. Quote it exactly.
5. Anything that broke: errors, a stuck screen, a refused action you expected to work. Note
   the in-game date shown on the top bar.
