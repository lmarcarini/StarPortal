# Playtest guide: M1.5

You run Site CAIRN, a base built around an alien Ring. You open wormholes by dialing addresses,
and you have to work every address out from the documents you are given. This build has four
story puzzles. This guide shows you the controls and nothing else: it gives no answers.

Plan on 30 to 60 minutes. Keep notes as you play (see "What to report" at the end).

## 1. Start a new game

Each time you launch the game, it starts a **new game**, paused. Saves from earlier builds won't
work with this one: their galaxy is different. **Don't press Load (F9) unless you saved in this
build.** Save (F5) and Load (F9) on the top bar use one quick-save slot.

The top bar also holds the clock controls. **Resume** / **Pause** is Space or Start on a gamepad.
**Faster** and **Slower** are `=` and `-`, or the right and left triggers. Time only passes
while the clock runs. Most waiting goes fastest at the top speed (10×).

There are two tabs under the top bar: **Gate Room** (F1) and **Archive** (F2). Q and E, or the
gamepad shoulder buttons, switch between them. The game opens in the Gate Room.

## 2. Install the capacitor bank

The Ring cannot dial without stored energy, and the base starts with none. Under the dial
console, the **Capacitors** gauge reads "No capacitors installed." Hold **Install capacitor bank
(6 h)** until its bar fills. Every costly button works this way: a quick press does nothing.

The install takes 6 in-game hours, and the clock has to run: press **Resume**. The gauge shows
"Installing: … left". Once the bank is in, the gauge shows its stored energy "of 400.0 MWh".
The base's plant charges it slowly.

## 3. Read the documents in the Archive

Press F2. A new game holds two documents, both marked **New**. Choose one to read it. The reader
shows its title, "Source: …", "Received …" and the text. When a document has a picture, press it
(or **View image**) to open it large. **Zoom in**, **Zoom out** and **Fit** are there, and so is
**Close**. Drag or use the arrows to move around, and the mouse wheel zooms. **All kinds**
filters the list by kind.

**Read two together:** with one document open, press **Read with…** and choose a second one. The
document it belongs with comes first, marked "(read together)". The two open side by side. The
second has its own **Close**.

New documents arrive here later, and they are marked **New** too. The game pauses when they
arrive.

## 4. Enter an address on the dial console

Press F1. The console has eight seals in a row, with the 36-glyph palette below them.

- **Seals 1 to 7 are yours.** You can drag a glyph onto a seal. You can also click a glyph
  ("… picked: choose a seal.") and then a seal. On a gamepad, press A on a glyph, then A on a
  seal.
- **Seal 8 fills itself.** It is always CAIRN's own mark, and the palette shows that glyph
  locked. Never try to enter it.
- Placed glyphs are highlighted on the palette. Placing a glyph that is already in another seal
  moves it.
- Right-click, Delete or Backspace clears a seal. Escape or gamepad B drops a picked glyph, or
  clears the focused seal when nothing is picked. **Clear all** empties all seven.

Once all seven are filled, hold **Hold to dial**. The gauge never shows how much energy an
address needs. If the bank is short, the Ring refuses ("Not enough charge. The Ring would not
turn."), spends nothing, and you can wait and try again. An address that has already failed
asks you to hold a second time, **Hold again to dial anyway**, because every dial spends energy.

## 5. The dialing sequence

A **Dialing** screen lights the eight seals one by one ("Seals engaging: … of 8"). **Skip**
(Escape, gamepad B) jumps straight to the result, which is the same either way.

- **No gate**, **Blocked** or **Origin rejected**: a short card says what that tells you and
  how much energy the dial cost. Press **OK**. A wrong address costs a small part of the
  charge, not all of it.
- **Lock** or **Hostile lock**: the wormhole screen opens.

## 6. The wormhole screen

It shows **Wormhole open**, the destination, the time left, the power drain and the state of
the capacitors. Below that is **What you can do now**:

- **Read the far Ring's console** copies 2 to 4 nearby addresses into your Address Book. They
  are already written for CAIRN. Their worlds stay unknown until you lock them. Each console
  can be read once.
- **Open the Address Book** steps away to the Gate Room. A bar at the top, **Wormhole open (…
  left): back to it**, brings you back. The screen comes back by itself when time or charge is
  about to run out.
- **Close wormhole** closes it. A wormhole also closes by itself when its time runs out.
  **Send the MOTH probe** stays disabled in this build.

The **first time** you lock a story world, the Gate Room's pole camera pushes through the
horizon. Its horizon record, and any documents the lock releases, go to the **Archive**, marked
**New**. The wormhole screen doesn't tell you about them, so check the Archive after every new
lock. Once the wormhole is closed, **Back to the console** returns you to the Gate Room.

## 7. The Address Book

The Address Book sits beside the console. It lists every address you have dialed and every
address a console gave you, newest first. Each row shows its source ("Dialed", "Console of …")
and its world, or "world unknown". Addresses from documents are not filled in for you: you
enter those yourself.

- **The glyph filter** is a small grid of all 36 glyphs. Press glyphs to show only the addresses
  that hold every glyph you pressed. Press a glyph again to remove it.
- **Search notes and worlds** filters by text. The source menu (**All sources**, **Documents**,
  **Consoles**, **Dialed**) shows one source. **Clear filters** resets them all.
- Select a row and press **Load** to put its address into the console. Type a note below the
  list and press **Save note** to keep it.

The **Dial Log** next to it records every dial you have made and what it gave.

## What to report

Write down what you did, what you expected and what happened. The most useful notes:

1. **Was the first puzzle obvious?** From its two documents alone, did you know what to enter
   and in what order? Where did you hesitate?
2. **Was the second puzzle fair?** Once you had the note and the rubbing side by side, did the
   answer feel earned, or guessed?
3. **Did you understand what to do after a lock?** Did you find the new documents? Did you read
   the console? Did you know what the Address Book entries were for?
4. The third and fourth puzzles: where you got stuck, and for how long.
5. Any label, button or message that confused you. Quote it exactly.
6. Anything that broke: errors, a stuck screen, time not moving, a dial refused that you expected
   to work. Note the in-game date shown on the top bar.

If you get stuck for more than 15 minutes, write down where, and ask for a hint.
