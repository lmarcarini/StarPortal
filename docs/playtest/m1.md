# M1 playtest: The Ring

The goal is to work out three gate addresses from clues alone, then dial each one and get
a lock. This should take about 20 to 40 minutes. Nobody expects you to know the hidden
address rule; the clues should be enough.

## Start a game

1. Open the project in the Godot editor and press **F5** (Run Project).
2. Every run starts a new game with new targets and is paused at 1 January 2026, 00:00.

Inside the game, **F5** is quick save and **F9** is quick load. A new day also autosaves.

## Time controls

The bar at the top shows the date and speed.

- **Space** pauses and resumes.
- **+** speeds up and **-** slows down: 0.5×, 1×, 3× or 10× (at 10× an in-game hour takes
  6 seconds).
- The game pauses itself whenever a dossier arrives.

## Where the clues appear

Press Space to start. Dossier 1 arrives at once, dossier 2 at the start of day 2 and
dossier 3 at the start of day 3. Each dossier points at one world.

Open the **Analysis** view: press **F2**, or **Q** / **E** to switch views. The clue cards
appear on the **Analysis Board** on the right. Select a card to read it. You can tag cards,
link them and mark one as suspect. All M1 clues are true.

## Narrow it down in the Workbench

The **Address Workbench** sits on the left of the Analysis view. It is being built in a
parallel card, so it may look a little different from this description.

- Eight slots: seals 1 to 7, then the origin. The origin is filled in for you with CAIRN's
  origin glyph.
- Drag a glyph from the palette into a seal, or click it and then click the seal. You can
  also rule glyphs out, mark them as in the address, or say "A is nearer than B".
- The counter shows how many addresses still fit. If it shows 0, one of your constraints is
  wrong.
- Once 50 or fewer fit, they are listed with the charge each one needs. Press **Send to dial
  queue** on the one you want to try.

Every dossier can be brought down to three addresses or fewer. Dialing settles the rest.

## Dial in the Gate Room

Press **F1** for the Gate Room.

1. Pick an address from the **Dial queue**, or type one in under "Enter an address".
2. The **Capacitors** gauge shows the stored charge, with a marker for what this dial needs.
   The bank starts empty and the plant charges it at 30 MW, so the first dial takes about
   10 in-game hours of waiting. Let time run.
3. **Hold** the dial button (about half a second) to dial. The seals take two in-game
   minutes to engage.
4. Read the result. A wrong address spends only part of the charge. A lock spends all of
   it, and the open wormhole draws more power while it stays open. Press **Close wormhole** in
   the Wormhole panel to close it; otherwise it closes itself after an hour.

The **Dial Log** keeps every attempt.

## What the outcomes mean

| Outcome | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **No gate** | There is no Ring at those coordinates: the address is wrong. Try the next candidate. |
| **Blocked** | A Ring is there, so the coordinates are right, but it is buried, busy or shielded. |
| **Origin rejected** | The last seal is not CAIRN's origin glyph. Fix the origin slot. |
| **Lock** | The wormhole is open. You found it. |
| **Hostile lock** | Open, but the far side is guarded. None of the M1 targets are guarded. |

In this build, all three targets usually start with the same seal-1 glyph, because only one
anchor is close enough for the starting bank. This is intended: neighbouring worlds look
alike.

## What to report back

- Did you lock all three targets? For each one: how many dials it took, and which in-game day.
- Which clues helped most, and which ones confused you or read badly?
- Where you got stuck, and for how long.
- Did the wait for charge feel fair, too long or too short?
- Anything broken: errors in the editor's Output panel, wrong counts, text cut off, controls
  that didn't respond. Include a screenshot if you can.
- If you used a quick save, mention it, and keep the save file. On Windows it is under
  `%APPDATA%\Godot\app_userdata\Far Side Protocol\saves`.
