# Playtest guide: M4.7, "Event time"

Events no longer change your speed. You choose a **cruise speed**; while something needs your
eyes the game runs slow (1×) and goes back to your cruise speed by itself when it is over.
Plan on 20 to 30 minutes. Saves from M4.6 load (they migrate to format 18).

## What changed

- **Cruise speed.** The clock on the top bar always shows the speed you chose. Nothing but you
  changes it.
- **Slow lamp.** Beside the clock, while the game is slowed: `SLOW · gate`, `dialling`,
  `conversation`, `call waiting`, `decision waiting` or `intruders`. An amber triangle means
  you may override it; a red square means you cannot.
- **What slows the game.**
  - Dialling, from the first seal to the lock or failure.
  - The gate operations panel (the action panel of an open wormhole).
  - A conversation, from answering to the end. Cannot be overridden.
  - Anything waiting for an answer: an incoming wormhole, a phone call, a missed check-in
    until you give the Signal.
  - Intruders (M4c, not yet). Cannot be overridden.
- **Faster while slowed** overrides every slow you may override and returns to cruise speed.
  That event does not slow again. Its tooltip says so, or says why it cannot.
- **On a lock** the game opens the Gate Room with the operations panel up. **Stand by** (or
  Esc / B, or leaving the tab) closes the panel and keeps the wormhole open at cruise speed.
  Clicking the portal on the Ring brings the panel, and the slow, back. While standing by, an
  alert "Wormhole open" stays on the alert list.
- During a conversation a lock does not switch tab; only the alert shows.
- The setting `gameplay/switch_to_gate_on_lock` (on by default) turns the automatic switch off;
  then nothing slows until you open the panel yourself.

## What to try

1. Set 10×. Dial a world you know. Watch the lamp say `dialling`, then `gate` on the lock.
2. Take the pole camera, then **Stand by**: back to 10×, wormhole still open, alert shown.
3. Click the portal: the panel and the slow come back. Switch to Base: back to 10×.
4. Wait for (or cheat in) a phone call: `call waiting`. Answer: `conversation`. Hang up: 10×.
5. Let an incoming wormhole run out without answering: 10× once it gives up.
6. With a call waiting, press Faster: 10× while it still waits.

## Questions for the owner

1. Is **1×** still needed as a speed the player picks, now that every event slows to 1× on its
   own? It could go, leaving 0.5×, 3× and 10×, or stay for players who like to watch.
2. Should anything else slow the game (research done, a team check-in, a hazard, a site
   finished)? Or stop slowing it (dialling, for routine dial-homes)?
3. Is a waiting incoming wormhole or call right to slow the game, or should it only pause on
   arrival as before and then run at cruise speed?

## What to report

Any moment the game is slow and the lamp does not say why, any time the speed you chose changed
without you, and anything on the gate panel that is hard to reach with keyboard or gamepad.
