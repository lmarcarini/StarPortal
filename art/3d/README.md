# 3D assets

All CC0 (public domain), by Quaternius (https://quaternius.com), supplied by the owner on
2026-11-16. Textures were downscaled to at most 1024 px; models are unchanged.

- `megakit/`: Modular Sci-Fi MegaKit (Standard): walls, platforms, columns, decals and
  props; aliens left out. Flattened so each glTF finds its textures beside it.
- `essentials/`: a subset of the Sci-Fi Essentials Kit (Standard): crates, desks, chairs,
  lockers, shelves, barrels, a mug and a satellite dish.

No characters are included in these packs.

`textures/`: CC0 PBR sets from ambientCG for the Gate hall (see its README.txt).
